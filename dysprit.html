About The Project

Dysprit is a Ukrainian online store for silver jewelry, delivered in a dark, elegant aesthetic. The catalog is structured into clear sections for rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, sets, and gifts — making it easy for shoppers to navigate and find the right piece.

Built on the Horoshop.ua e-commerce platform with a fully custom design integration. The result is a refined shopping experience that matches the premium feel of the brand.

Catalog Sections

  1. Каблучки (Rings). A curated selection of silver rings — from minimalist bands to ornate statement pieces.
  2. Сережки (Earrings). Studs, hoops, and drop earrings covering both everyday and evening styles.
  3. Намиста (Necklaces). Chains and pendants with attention to weight, length, and detail.
  4. Браслети (Bracelets). From delicate chain links to bolder wrist pieces.
  5. Набори (Sets). Coordinated jewelry sets — gift-ready and consistent in style.
  6. Подарунки (Gifts). Pre-selected gift options that take the guesswork out of choosing.
