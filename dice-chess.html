About The Game

Dice Battle Chess Kingdom brings a fresh, dynamic twist to traditional chess. Instead of standard pieces, you control an army of dice warriors. The value rolled on the top face of the die determines how far your warrior can move across the battlefield.

It requires careful planning, risk management, and strategic positioning to outmaneuver your opponent and claim victory.

How to Play

  1. Move dice by rolling them. The top face of the die shows exactly how many cells you can move in a single turn.
  2. Capture enemies by landing on them. Standard capturing rules apply. Remember, the King piece is special and can only move 1 cell at a time.
  3. Win the match by either capturing the enemy King or successfully moving your own King onto the enemy's Palace square.
