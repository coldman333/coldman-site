About The Project

Auto News Portal is a cutting-edge digital publishing platform tailored for the electric vehicle industry. The platform focuses on delivery of high-quality content, speed, and a seamless reading experience.

By leveraging a modern headless architecture, Auto News Portal ensures lightning-fast page loads and an intuitive administrative interface for managing a vast library of automotive news.

Key Features

  1. Dynamic Content Delivery. An optimized reading experience designed to provide the latest EV news with minimal latency.
  2. Robust Content Management. Powered by Payload CMS, providing full control over articles, categories, and featured news without touching code.
  3. Premium Responsive Design. Built with Tailwind CSS, the site delivers a flawless experience across all devices, ensuring news is accessible everywhere.
